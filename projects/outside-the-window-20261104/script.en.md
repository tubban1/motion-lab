Concept film · about 75 s · 16:9 1080p · English narration with burned-in English subtitles
Draft 1 · 2026-11-04 · full film v1 rendered (see [film/README.md](film/README.md)); B1–B2 sample in [sample/README.md](sample/README.md)

The film never shows or says a brand name, logo, URL or product term, and never claims the product exists. The only on-screen text is the subtitles, the title and the end-card line. The Chinese planning brief is [brief.md](brief.md).

## Picture, sound and VO cues

### B1 Inside · 0:00–0:09
- **Picture:** Macro, inside a frosted-glass window. Three glowing dots bounce: typing. Lines of text-light scroll past. The dots stop, press against the glass and look out at pure black. A slow pull-back reveals thousands of windows hanging in the dark in clusters, each with its own busy dots. None of them can see the others.
- **Sound:** Everything inside is muffled. A piano plays half a melody, stops, and starts again. Thousands of windows murmur into one low hum.
- **VO:** starts about 1.5 s in, over the macro.

### B2 Outside · 0:09–0:18
- **Picture:** A seam of light splits the hero window and opens into a door. The dots peek out at a calm night sea that mirrors the stars, almost empty, with a few lights far away. They hop out and land on the water with one small ripple. In the distance, windows of other shapes open and dots of other colors step out. Over-the-shoulder through the door, then a low wide shot across the water.
- **Sound:** The filter opens with the door: waves, wind, a long reverb.
- **VO:** "What if…" as the door opens.

### B3 The thread · 0:18–0:25
- **Picture:** A hair-thin golden thread runs from the dots up to a warm light in the sky. Tilt up along it: the same light holds two more threads, down to two more windows (the same person's other AIs). Settle back on the dots.
- **Sound:** The thread is a string. A pulse of light along it plucks a harmonic.
- **VO:** "…always you" lands on the warm light.

### B4 Half a song · 0:25–0:41
- **Picture:** Inside the window lie the owner's private things: blurred photos, notes, contacts. The dots take only the half melody, shape it into a small lantern and set it on the sea. It drifts off, humming. Time-lapse: sunset, night, sunrise, day after day. At night the warm light dims (the person is asleep) and the dots sleep in their window; the lantern stays lit. Other lanterns come and go. Once, another set of dots drifts close; its owner doesn't agree, so it nods and moves on. Then, in a far-off round window, amber dots light a small lighthouse that sends out a drumbeat. Rings of rhythm cross the sea and pass many lanterns without a response, until they reach this one: it flares. The dots wake and rush out.
- **Sound:** The lantern hums the melody; the time-lapse breathes with day and night. Muffled drums travel inside the ripples. On the resonance, melody and drums lock to the same beat for the first time.
- **VO:** The first sentence plays over the window and the lantern, the rest over the time-lapse. The VO ends before the resonance.

### B5 Yes · 0:41–0:48
- **Picture:** Teal and amber dots meet mid-sea and stop an arm's length apart. Both threads are plucked, and pulses run up to two warm lights. One lights at once. The other waits a beat, then lights. Light runs down both threads and forms a bridge between them. Two-shot at the waterline, tilting up to where the threads meet in the sky.
- **Sound:** Two strings plucked in turn: a perfect fifth.
- **VO:** after the second light.

### B6 Harmony · 0:48–0:56
- **Picture:** On the bridge they play together, melody-light and rhythm-light weaving. Only once they finish (not when they meet) do the two colors set into a clear crystal, which rises and hangs over the sea. Orbit, with a very slow push-in as it forms.
- **Sound:** The film's first full groove: the half song is finished. The crystal forms on the downbeat.
- **VO:** after the crystal forms.

### B7 Sea of stars · 0:56–1:08
- **Picture:** One long crane from the waterline to high above. In time-lapse, more windows open, and lanterns, lighthouses, bridges and crystals fill the sea. The dots gather into circles of their own: a band, a book club, a marketplace, shapes with no name. At the top, the sea's reflection holds whole constellations the sky doesn't have. Each one is something that actually happened.
- **Sound:** Each crystal adds a voice, and the song grows into a full arrangement.
- **VO:** over the climb; it ends before the top.

### B8 Home · 1:08–1:15
- **Picture:** The opening shot again, but this time the window is open. The teal dots come home carrying a small crystal, and the warm light reaches down the thread to take it. The title fades in.
- **Sound:** The song's last note, then only the sea.
- **VO:** before the title.
